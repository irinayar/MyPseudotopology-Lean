# Lean formalization notes for the fourth article on pseudotopological groups

Modules: `LeanCheck/PGFourGroupwise.lean`, `PGFourCover.lean` and the axiom report `PGFourAxioms.lean`. They build on `PGThreeFamilies` and earlier modules (see `PGTHREE_FORMALIZATION.md`). Typechecked with Lean 4.35.0 and Mathlib (the `lean-toolchain` of this repository), one module at a time; no `sorry`; every declaration listed in `PGFourAxioms.lean` depends only on `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`. A Lean proof verifies its formal statement under its hypotheses; the correspondence with the article requires mathematical review.

## `PGFourGroupwise` (27 November 2026)

`Meets J` says that any family, indexed by `J`, of groupwise dense families has a common member. It stands for "`J` has fewer elements than the groupwise density number".

| Declaration | Statement |
|---|---|
| `above_dense` | For a nonprincipal ultrafilter `v` and a function `h`, the sets whose next-element function exceeds `h` on a member of `v` form a groupwise dense family. |
| `above_cofinal` | Hence no family indexed by `J` is cofinal in the ultrapower by `v` (a theorem of Blass and Mildenberger). |
| `common_pfilter_of_groupwise` | The common members of P-points indexed by `J` form a P-filter. |
| `pfilter_modification_of_groupwise` | Openness for the modification of the PG generated by all ultrafilters containing every common member is openness for a group topology, the finest group topology of the PG of the P-points. |
| `exists_open_not_nhds_of_copies` | If functions indexed by `J` are cofinal in the ultrapower by `v`, the copies of `v` placed on their graphs and mapped into `ℤ` by a rapidly growing sequence generate a PG whose modification is not given by a group topology. |

Not formalized: the cardinal invariants themselves, that the copies are pairwise different and isomorphic to `v`, and any consistency statement.

## `PGFourCover` (27 November 2026)

`NotFinDom Y` says that `Y` is not finitely dominating. `Uncovered J` says that sets indexed by `J`, none finitely dominating, do not cover all functions. It stands for "`J` has fewer elements than `cov(D_fin)`".

| Declaration | Statement |
|---|---|
| `notFinDom_iff` | `Y` is not finitely dominating exactly when all its members lie below one function on a member of one nonprincipal ultrafilter. |
| `uncovered_of_meets`, `uncovered_of_bounded` | `cov(D_fin)` is at least the groupwise density number and at least the bounding number. |
| `common_pfilter_of_uncovered` | The common members of P-points indexed by `J` form a P-filter. |
| `pfilter_modification_of_uncovered` | Openness for the modification of the PG generated by all ultrafilters containing every common member is openness for a group topology, the finest group topology of the PG of the P-points. |

These statements contain the earlier ones for `Meets J`. Not formalized: the cardinal invariants themselves and any consistency statement.
