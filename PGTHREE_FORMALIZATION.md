# PGthree: Lean checks (25 November 2026)

Modules: `LeanCheck/PGThreeSelective.lean`, `PGThreeSelectiveCH.lean`, `PGThreeExact.lean`, `PGThreeSparse.lean`, `PGThreeModification.lean`, `PGThreePPoint.lean`, `PGThreeNull.lean`, `PGThreeGeneral.lean`, and the axiom report `PGThreeAxioms.lean`. They build on modules of the first two articles (see `PG_FORMALIZATION.md`, `PGTWO_FORMALIZATION.md`). Typechecked with Lean 4.35.0 and Mathlib (the `lean-toolchain` of this repository), one module at a time; no `sorry`; every declaration listed in `PGThreeAxioms.lean` depends only on `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`. A Lean proof verifies its formal statement under its hypotheses; the correspondence with the statements of the article (`PGthree`, Pseudotopological Groups III, unpublished) was checked by the author and has not been reviewed independently. No independent build of these modules by anyone else has been made.

Statement numbers below are those of Sections 3 to 8 of the article.

Setting. `IsSel u` : the ultrafilter `u` is nonprincipal and every function to `ℕ` is constant or injective on a member of `u`. `Iso u v` : `v` is the image of `u` under a permutation. The PG generated by a set `S` of ultrafilters is `genFam (ultraGen S)` (`PGTwoGeneration`). The continuum hypothesis is the explicit hypothesis `𝔠 = ℵ₁`.

## `PGThreeSparse` (Section 5)
Hypotheses as in `condM_of_growth` (`PGTwoDigits`): `y` positive, for every `B` eventually `2 B (y 0 + ... + y (l-1)) < y l`, and the ultrafilter `u` on `ℤ` contains the finite sums of every tail of `y`. No set-theoretic hypothesis appears.

| Declaration | Statement |
|---|---|
| `exists_weight` | A nondecreasing unbounded `β` with `2 β l (y 0 + ... + y (l-1)) < y l` for all `l`. |
| `lt_abs_val`, `val_inj` | Expansions with coefficients bounded by `β` are unique. |
| `sparse_strict` | In the PG generated by `u` the only null principal ultrafilter is `pure 0`; some member `A` of the vicinity filter contains no set that is open for the topological modification and contains `0`; no member `B` of the vicinity filter has `B + B ⊆ A`; the vicinity filter is not null. |

## `PGThreeNull` (Section 6, no set-theoretic hypothesis except (S) in the corollary)
`G` is a countably infinite abelian group with finitely many solutions of `m • x = 0` for every `m ≥ 1`, `S` a set of pairwise non-isomorphic selective ultrafilters, `pw w` the sum in the semigroup of ultrafilters of the images `c • u` for the letters `(u, c)` of the word `w`.

| Declaration | Statement |
|---|---|
| `null_free_iff`, `pw_injective` | Theorem 6.1: the nonprincipal ultrafilters converging to `0` are exactly the `pw w` for nonempty words with letters in `S` and nonzero coefficients, and different words give different ultrafilters. |
| `only_trivial`, `kernel_open` | Theorem 6.2, for every such `S` whatever its size: the only neighbourhood filter of a group topology among the null filters is `pure 0`; continuous homomorphisms from topological groups have open kernels; for nonempty `S` the vicinity filter is not null. The proof uses an idempotent in a compact semigroup of ultrafilters. |
| `exists_discrete_coreflection` | Corollary 6.3, under (S). |

## `PGThreeGeneral` (Sections 6 and 8 on every countably infinite abelian group)
`G` is any countably infinite abelian group and `S` a set of pairwise non-isomorphic selective ultrafilters whose PG has unique limits, in the equivalent form that every image `m • u` is nonprincipal or `pure 0`.

| Declaration | Statement |
|---|---|
| `null_free_iff_general`, `pw_injective_general` | Theorem 6.1: the nonprincipal null ultrafilters are the `pw w` for nonempty words none of whose images `c • u` is `pure 0`, and `pw w` determines the list of images. |
| `only_trivial_general`, `kernel_open_general` | Theorem 6.2. |
| `exists_discrete_coreflection_general` | Corollary 6.3, when every equation `m • x = 0` has finitely many solutions or holds everywhere, under (S). |
| `isOpenMod_compl_singleton`, `ppoint_hausdorff` | Section 8: in a PG with unique limits points are closed for the modification; so for countably many P-points whose PG has unique limits the group topology separates points. |

## `PGThreeModification` (Section 7, no set-theoretic hypothesis)
Here `u j` (`j` in a countable index type) are pairwise non-isomorphic selective ultrafilters on a countably infinite abelian group `G`, `e : ℕ ≃ G`, `pos S y` is the number of elements of `S` that are at most `y`, and `Wset e S` is the set of the finite sums `∑ c y • e y` with `|c y| ≤ pos (S j) y` for some `j` with `y ∈ S j`.

| Declaration | Statement |
|---|---|
| `exists_thinned`, `exists_halved` | Lemma 7.1: the sets `S j` can be shrunk so that the sum of two elements of the new basic set lies in the old one. |
| `exists_basic_subset` | Lemma 7.2: a set `O` containing `0` with `{y \| x + c • e y ∈ O} ∈ e⁻¹(u j)` for all `x ∈ O`, `c`, `j` contains a basic set. |
| `modification_isGroupTopology` | Theorem 7.3: openness for the topological modification of the PG generated by the `u j` is openness for a group topology; it is the finest group topology of the PG; its neighbourhoods of `0` are the sets containing a basic set; and it separates points when only finitely many `x` satisfy `m • x = 0` for each `m ≥ 1`. |
| `exists_sparse_of_selective`, `selective_int_strict` | Corollary 7.4: for a selective ultrafilter on `ℤ` the modification is a Hausdorff group topology, and some vicinity set is not one of its neighbourhoods of `0` and contains no `B + B` with `B` a vicinity set. |

## `PGThreePPoint` (Section 8, no set-theoretic hypothesis)
`IsPPoint u` : `u` is nonprincipal and every function to `ℕ` none of whose fibres is in `u` is finite-to-one on a member of `u`. `Uset A` is the set of the finite sums with one term, or its negative, or `0`, from each of `A 0, ..., A (n-1)`.

| Declaration | Statement |
|---|---|
| `IsPPoint.pseudoInter` | Lemma 8.1. |
| `exists_Uset_subset`, `ppoint_modification` | Theorem 8.2: for countably many P-points on a countably infinite abelian group, openness for the topological modification of the PG they generate is openness for a group topology, which is the finest one; the sets `Uset A`, with every `A i` in every ultrafilter, form a base of its neighbourhoods of `0`. |
| `exists_open_not_nhds` | Theorem 8.3: for a rapidly growing sequence `y` and a free ultrafilter `w` on `ℕ` that is not a P-point, some set that is open for the modification and contains `0` is a neighbourhood of `0` in no group topology in which the image of `w` converges to `0`. |
| `modification_isGroupTopology_iff` | Corollary 8.4: in that situation the modification is a group topology if and only if `w` is a P-point. |


Not formalized: Remark 3.9 (earlier work of the literature) and the second part of Remark 4.6 (the comparison with groups whose Zariski topology is cofinite).

The proof of Lemma 3.6 differs from the article's: the article builds `2^𝔠` selective ultrafilters inside every infinite set by a binary tree, while `exists_selective_avoiding` builds one by a single tower of length `ω₁` that avoids the given ones. That every infinite set belongs to `2^𝔠` selective ultrafilters is therefore not formalized.
