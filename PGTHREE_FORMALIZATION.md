# PGthree: Lean checks (25 November 2026)

Modules: `LeanCheck/PGThreeSelective.lean`, `PGThreeSelectiveCH.lean`, `PGThreeExact.lean`, and the axiom report `PGThreeAxioms.lean`. They build on modules of the first two articles (see `PG_FORMALIZATION.md`, `PGTWO_FORMALIZATION.md`). Typechecked with Lean 4.35.0 and Mathlib (the `lean-toolchain` of this repository), one module at a time; no `sorry`; every declaration listed in `PGThreeAxioms.lean` depends only on `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`. A Lean proof verifies its formal statement under its hypotheses; the correspondence with the statements of the article (`PGthree`, Pseudotopological Groups III, unpublished) was checked by the author and has not been reviewed independently. No independent build of these modules by anyone else has been made.

Statement numbers below are those of Sections 3 and 4 of the article.

Setting. `IsSel u` : the ultrafilter `u` is nonprincipal and every function to `ℕ` is constant or injective on a member of `u`. `Iso u v` : `v` is the image of `u` under a permutation. The PG generated by a set `S` of ultrafilters is `genFam (ultraGen S)` (`PGTwoGeneration`). The continuum hypothesis is the explicit hypothesis `𝔠 = ℵ₁`.

## `PGThreeExact` (Section 4)
`Supply` is the statement (S): there is a set of at least `𝔠` pairwise non-isomorphic selective ultrafilters on `ℕ`. `TowerProp`: every family of fewer than `𝔠` infinite subsets of an infinite set, nested modulo finite sets, has an infinite pseudointersection; it holds if `𝔭 = 𝔠`.

| Declaration | Statement |
|---|---|
| `dense_iff_supply` | Lemma 4.1 for subsets of `ℕ` (the transport to a countably infinite group is made inside `exists_vicinity_of_condition`). |
| `towerProp_of_CH`, `supply_of_towerProp`, `supply_of_CH` | Remark 4.2. `𝔭` itself is not defined; the implication from `𝔭 = 𝔠` to `TowerProp` is the definition of `𝔭`. The case `cov(M) = 𝔠`, quoted in the article, is not formalized. |
| `genFam_hausdorff_iff` | Proposition 4.3, for all nonzero integers `m`. |
| `finite_or_zero_of_vicinity` | Theorem 4.4(a), for any abelian group and any subgroup, with no set-theoretic hypothesis. |
| `exists_vicinity_of_condition` | Theorem 4.4(b). |
| `not_vicinity_boolean_times_int` | Corollary 4.5, on `(ℕ →₀ ZMod 2) × ℤ`: the cofinite filter at `0` is not the vicinity filter of any PG. |
| `exists_vicinity_boolean` | The first sentence of Remark 4.6. |

Not formalized: Remark 3.9 (earlier work of the literature) and the second part of Remark 4.6 (the comparison with groups whose Zariski topology is cofinite).

The proof of Lemma 3.6 differs from the article's: the article builds `2^𝔠` selective ultrafilters inside every infinite set by a binary tree, while `exists_selective_avoiding` builds one by a single tower of length `ω₁` that avoids the given ones. That every infinite set belongs to `2^𝔠` selective ultrafilters is therefore not formalized.
