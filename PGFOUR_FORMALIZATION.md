# Lean formalization notes for the fourth article on pseudotopological groups

Modules: `LeanCheck/PGFourGroupwise.lean`, `PGFourCover.lean`, `PGFourSums.lean`, `PGFourClassify.lean`, `PGFourFinite.lean`, `PGFourVicinity.lean` and the axiom report `PGFourAxioms.lean`. They build on `PGThreeFamilies` and earlier modules (see `PGTHREE_FORMALIZATION.md`). Typechecked with Lean 4.35.0 and Mathlib (the `lean-toolchain` of this repository), one module at a time; no `sorry`; every declaration listed in `PGFourAxioms.lean` depends only on `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`. A Lean proof verifies its formal statement under its hypotheses; the correspondence with the article requires mathematical review.

## `PGFourGroupwise` (27 November 2026)

`Meets J` says that any family, indexed by `J`, of groupwise dense families has a common member. It stands for "`J` has fewer elements than the groupwise density number".

| Declaration | Statement |
|---|---|
| `above_dense` | For a nonprincipal ultrafilter `v` and a function `h`, the sets whose next-element function exceeds `h` on a member of `v` form a groupwise dense family. |
| `above_cofinal` | Hence no family indexed by `J` is cofinal in the ultrapower by `v` (a theorem of Blass and Mildenberger). |
| `common_pfilter_of_groupwise` | The common members of P-points indexed by `J` form a P-filter. |
| `pfilter_modification_of_groupwise` | Openness for the modification of the PG generated by all ultrafilters containing every common member is openness for a group topology, the finest group topology of the PG of the P-points. |
| `exists_open_not_nhds_of_copies` | If functions indexed by `J` are cofinal in the ultrapower by `v`, the copies of `v` placed on their graphs and mapped into `ℤ` by a rapidly growing sequence generate a PG whose modification is not given by a group topology. |

Not formalized: the cardinal invariants themselves, that the copies are pairwise different and isomorphic to `v`, and any consistency statement.

## `PGFourCover` (27 November 2026)

`NotFinDom Y` says that `Y` is not finitely dominating. `Uncovered J` says that sets indexed by `J`, none finitely dominating, do not cover all functions. It stands for "`J` has fewer elements than `cov(D_fin)`".

| Declaration | Statement |
|---|---|
| `notFinDom_iff` | `Y` is not finitely dominating exactly when all its members lie below one function on a member of one nonprincipal ultrafilter. |
| `uncovered_of_meets`, `uncovered_of_bounded` | `cov(D_fin)` is at least the groupwise density number and at least the bounding number. |
| `common_pfilter_of_uncovered` | The common members of P-points indexed by `J` form a P-filter. |
| `pfilter_modification_of_uncovered` | Openness for the modification of the PG generated by all ultrafilters containing every common member is openness for a group topology, the finest group topology of the PG of the P-points. |

These statements contain the earlier ones for `Meets J`. Not formalized: the cardinal invariants themselves and any consistency statement.

## `PGFourSums` (27 November 2026)

`y` is a rapidly growing sequence of positive integers, `v` is any nonprincipal ultrafilter on `ℕ`, and `sumU y v k` is the ultrafilter sum of `k` copies of the image of `v` under `y`. No set-theoretic hypothesis is used.

| Declaration | Statement |
|---|---|
| `terms_mem`, `exists_terms` | The sums of `k` terms with large indices form a member; every member contains a sum of `k` terms with indices in a given member of `v`. |
| `exists_open_not_nhds` | For `k ≥ 2` there is a set containing `0`, open for the topological modification of the PG generated by `sumU y v k`, that is a neighbourhood of `0` in no group topology in which this ultrafilter converges to `0`. |
| `not_isGroupTopology` | Hence openness for the modification is not openness for a group topology. |

## `PGFourClassify` (27 November 2026)

`y` is a rapidly growing sequence of positive integers, `l` is a nonempty list of pairs `(c, v)` of a nonzero integer and a nonprincipal ultrafilter on `ℕ`, and `sumL y l` is the ultrafilter sum of the images of the `v` under `n ↦ c * y n`. No set-theoretic hypothesis is used.

| Declaration | Statement |
|---|---|
| `diff_small` | In every group topology in which `sumL y l` converges to `0`, the differences `d (y m - y m')` are small for `d` in the subgroup generated by the coefficients carried by one ultrafilter. |
| `not_group_of_sep` | The non-group case, under an explicit condition on the class sums. |
| `genFam_eq_of_one` | If all ultrafilters are `w` and the coefficients sum to `±d`, with `d` dividing each, then `sumL y l` and the image of `w` under `n ↦ d * y n` generate the same PG. |
| `classification` | The modification is a group topology exactly when all ultrafilters are one P-point `w` and the coefficients sum to `±d`, where `d > 0` generates the subgroup they generate. |

`PGFourSums` is the special case of equal coefficients `1`.

## `PGFourFinite` (27 November 2026)

A finite list `LL` of generators of the kind of `PGFourClassify`. `Lat y LL` is the group generated by their class sums `w ↦ S w l`, `W` is the set of ultrafilters that occur, and `d w` is a positive integer in the group generated by the coefficients carried by `w`, dividing all of them.

| Declaration | Statement |
|---|---|
| `not_group_of_sep_family` | The non-group case, under an explicit condition on the group of class sums. |
| `lattice_mem` | Every element of the group of class sums gives a null filter. |
| `classification_family` | The modification of the PG generated by the sums is a group topology exactly when every `w` in `W` is a P-point and `d w` times the `w`-th coordinate lies in the group of class sums. |

## `PGFourVicinity` (27 November 2026)

`E y C m` is the set of values of nonzero integer vectors supported above `m` with entries bounded by `C`.

| Declaration | Statement |
|---|---|
| `sparse_strict` | If every member of a nonempty set `U` of ultrafilters contains all `E y C m` (with `C` depending on the ultrafilter), then some set belonging to every filter that converges to `0` in the generated PG contains no set that contains `0` and is open for the topological modification. |

## `PGFourPretop` (27 November 2026)

Setting of `PGXi`: `ξ` is the PG of a Hausdorff group topology and an ideal `I` with (I1)-(I5).

| Declaration | Statement |
|---|---|
| `open_subset_of_pretopological` | If `ξ` is pretopological, every vicinity set contains a set that contains `0` and is open for the topological modification. |
| `sparse_of_not_pretopological` | If `I` is generated by an increasing sequence, the topology has countable tightness at `0` and `ξ` is not pretopological, then some vicinity set contains no such open set. |
| `pretopological_iff_open_base` | Under the same two hypotheses, the two properties are equivalent. |

## `PGFourPretopEx` (27 November 2026)

`G = ι →₀ ZMod 2` with `ι` uncountable.

| Namespace | Statement |
|---|---|
| `Countably` | Topology of pointwise convergence, sets supported in one countable set: admissible; countable tightness (`tight`); not pretopological; every vicinity set is a neighbourhood of `0`. |
| `Bounded` | Topology given by vanishing on countable sets, sets with supports of bounded size: Hausdorff group topology; admissible; generated by the increasing sets `A k`; not pretopological; every vicinity set is a neighbourhood of `0`. |

Not formalized: that the ideal of the first example is not countably generated, and that the topology of the second has uncountable tightness.

## `PGFourVicSeq` (27 November 2026)

`F n` are countably many filters with limits along a map `e : G → K`; `B0` is an infinite set whose infinite subsets contain two infinite sets with images of disjoint closures.

| Declaration | Statement |
|---|---|
| `exists_disjoint` | Every infinite subset of `B0` has an infinite subset missed by some member of `F n`. |
| `no_vicinity_sequence` | If no point of `B0` lies in every member of any `F n`, some infinite subset of `B0` is disjoint from a set that belongs to every `F n`. |

Not formalized: the existence of such a set `B0` (an `I₀` set, quoted), that the finite signed sums of the generators are the filters to which the statement is applied (the null ultrafilters of a generated PG), and the case of fewer than `𝔥` generators.

## `PGFourContinuum` (27 November 2026)

`genFam (ultraGen S)` is the family of filters converging to `0` in the PG generated by `S`.

| Declaration | Statement |
|---|---|
| `vicinity_no_sequence` | `S` of fewer than continuum many ultrafilters, `ξ(S)` Hausdorff, `e : G →+ K` into a compact Hausdorff group, `B0` an infinite set without `0` whose disjoint subsets have images with disjoint closures: some infinite `B ⊆ B0` has its complement in every filter converging to `0`. |
| `bound_sharp` | On `ℕ →₀ ZMod 2` some set of at most continuum many ultrafilters has `ξ(S)` Hausdorff, while no infinite set of unit vectors has its complement in every filter converging to `0`. |

Not formalized: the existence of such a set `B0` inside every infinite set (an `I₀` set, quoted), and the corollary on sequences and countable bases. This module supersedes the use of `PGFourVicSeq`, which treats countably many filters.

## `PGFourQuotient` (27 November 2026)

`ξ` is the PG of a topology and an ideal, `N` a subgroup, `q : G → G ⧸ N`. `quotC N (nullXi I)` is the family of filters converging to `0` in the final quotient, `nullTarget N I` the family of filters on `G ⧸ N` that contain the neighbourhoods of `0` and the image of a member of `I`.

| Declaration | Statement |
|---|---|
| `quot_subset` | The first family is contained in the second. |
| `quotient_eq_iff` | They are equal iff for every `A ∈ I` and every choice of neighbourhoods `U B` of `0`, one for each `B ∈ I`, finitely many of the sets `q '' (U B ∩ B)` cover the trace of a neighbourhood of `0` on `q '' A`. |
| `quotient_eq_of_compact_add` | They are equal if `N ⊆ K + M` with `K ⊆ N` compact and `M ⊆ N` a member of `I`. |
| `QuotEx.equal`, `QuotEx.no_single` | A case with `N` compact, so that the families are equal, in which no single member of `I` serves for all neighbourhoods. |

## `PGFourCoreflection` (27 November 2026)

`β` is the 2-adic topology of `ℤ` and `I` the ideal of the sets contained in `F + A m r`, built from the numbers `2 ^ (n ^ 2)` split into infinitely many columns.

| Declaration | Statement |
|---|---|
| `admissible`, `adic_t2` | `I` satisfies (I1)-(I5), and the 2-adic topology is Hausdorff. |
| `few_terms` | Only finitely many terms of column `m + 1` lie in `F + A m r` with `F` finite. |
| `col_converges` | Every filter convergent for the group topology of a column converges for the PG. |
| `no_coarsest` | There is no coarsest group topology all of whose convergent filters converge for the PG. |

Not formalized: that a coreflection exists exactly when there is such a coarsest topology (quoted).

## `PGFourOrder` (27 November 2026): the order of summation

In `PGFourClassify` and `PGFourFinite`, `sumL y l` takes the **last** entry of `l` outermost, while the article takes the first summand outermost (`A ∈ p + q` iff `{x | {z | x + z ∈ A} ∈ q} ∈ p`). Addition of ultrafilters is not commutative.

| Declaration | Statement |
|---|---|
| `psum`, `psum_eq` | The sum with the first entry outermost equals `sumL y l.reverse`. |
| `classification_psum`, `classification_family_psum` | The two classification theorems for `psum`, in the order of the article. |

Correspondence with the article should be read through these two declarations.

## `PGFourAllGroups` (27 November 2026)

`y` is a sparse sequence in an abelian group `G` (`Sparse`), `I y` the ideal of the sets contained in `F + A y m r`, and `top y univ` the group topology with the base of the sums of high terms with small digits.

| Declaration | Statement |
|---|---|
| `exists_sparse` | Every infinite abelian group has a sparse sequence. |
| `admissible`, `top_t2` | `I y` satisfies (I1)-(I5) and `top y univ` is Hausdorff. |
| `no_coarsest` | There is no coarsest group topology all of whose convergent filters converge for the PG. |
| `every_infinite_group` | The conclusion, for every infinite abelian group. |
| `no_ideal_of_finite` | A finite abelian group carries no ideal with (I1)-(I5). |

Not formalized: that a coreflection exists exactly when there is such a coarsest topology (quoted), the countable base of the topology and the countable generation of the ideal.
