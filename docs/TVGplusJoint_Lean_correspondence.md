# TVGplusJoint: correspondence between the claims and the Lean declarations (18 November 2026)

Repository `irinayar/MyPseudotopology-Lean`; modules read in the clone at commit `58dcbdb` (the TVGplus modules were added in `bf0f3b3` and `c03586b`; TPP modules in `15e216c` and `58dcbdb`). Axiom lines are copied from `verification/tvgplus-check.txt`, `verification/tvgshifted-check.txt` and `verification/tpp-check.txt`. A Lean proof verifies its formal statement under its hypotheses; this table lists the formal statements next to the statements of the article so that a reader can compare them. `Pn S k` is the pseudonorm `sup_{q in S} ||k/q||` over a set `S` of scales; `InBall S δ k` means `Pn (sqScales p) k < δ` for every `p ∈ S`; `DD S` is diagonal density.
