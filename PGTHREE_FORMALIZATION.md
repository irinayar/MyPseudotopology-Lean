# PGthree: Lean checks (25 November 2026)

Modules: `LeanCheck/PGThreeSelective.lean`, `PGThreeSelectiveCH.lean`, and the axiom report `PGThreeAxioms.lean`. They build on modules of the first two articles (see `PG_FORMALIZATION.md`, `PGTWO_FORMALIZATION.md`). Typechecked with Lean 4.35.0 and Mathlib (the `lean-toolchain` of this repository), one module at a time; no `sorry`; every declaration listed in `PGThreeAxioms.lean` depends only on `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`. A Lean proof verifies its formal statement under its hypotheses; the correspondence with the statements of the article (`PGthree`, Pseudotopological Groups III, unpublished) was checked by the author and has not been reviewed independently. No independent build of these modules by anyone else has been made.

Statement numbers below are those of Section 3 of the article.

Setting. `IsSel u` : the ultrafilter `u` is nonprincipal and every function to `ℕ` is constant or injective on a member of `u`. `Iso u v` : `v` is the image of `u` under a permutation. The PG generated by a set `S` of ultrafilters is `genFam (ultraGen S)` (`PGTwoGeneration`). The continuum hypothesis is the explicit hypothesis `𝔠 = ℵ₁`.

## `PGThreeSelectiveCH` (under `𝔠 = ℵ₁`)
| Declaration | Statement |
|---|---|
| `exists_selective_avoiding` | Every infinite set belongs to a selective ultrafilter different from `ℵ₁` given ones. |
| `exists_dense_selective` | Lemma 3.6: pairwise non-isomorphic selective ultrafilters dense in a given infinite set. |
| `exists_hausdorff_vicinity` | Theorem 3.7: for an infinite subgroup `H` there is a null family with unique limits whose vicinity filter consists of the sets containing `0` and almost all of `H`. |
| `exists_hausdorff_cofin_int` | Theorem 3.7 for `ℤ` and Corollary 3.8: a free ultrafilter is null, the open sets of the topological modification are the empty and cofinite sets, the neighbourhood filter of the finest group topology is `⊤`, and the vicinity filter is not null. |

The proof of Lemma 3.6 differs from the article's: the article builds `2^𝔠` selective ultrafilters inside every infinite set by a binary tree, while `exists_selective_avoiding` builds one by a single tower of length `ω₁` that avoids the given ones. That every infinite set belongs to `2^𝔠` selective ultrafilters is therefore not formalized.
