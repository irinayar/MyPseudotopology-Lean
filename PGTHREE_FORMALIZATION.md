| `common_pfilter` | For P-points indexed by a set such that every family of functions indexed by it is dominated by one function, the common members form a P-filter. |
| `pfilter_modification` | Then openness for the topological modification of the PG generated by all ultrafilters containing every common member is openness for a group topology, with an explicit base, and this is the finest group topology of the PG of the `w j`. |

Not formalized: that an injective image of a selective ultrafilter is selective, that non-isomorphism is preserved, that selective ultrafilters are rapid, and the cardinal invariants themselves (the hypotheses are stated by domination).
